## Implications for our refile
- (viii)/(ix): corroborate with independent documents (org charts, comparator wage data), not letters alone.
- (iv): document selective, repeated reviewing at SIGIR/KDD/CIKM/RecSys/ACM journals with invitation records and acceptance rates.
- (v): collect adoption by unaffiliated parties (dependents, third-party citations, downloads over time) to show sustained, not recent, acclaim.
- Given PA-2026-05, file complete and address prior final-merits findings head-on; avoid relying on a possible RFE to cure gaps.
- Watch the November bulletin for EB-1 India movement before mid-2027 filing.
