## Implications for our refile
- Final merits: preserve the record. *Mukherji* is a useful citation for a later federal challenge but does not change adjudication; build for two-step review anyway.
- (iv) Peer review: document selectivity and sustained timeline (SIGIR/KDD/CIKM/RecSys/ACM journals) across several years, not one season, to answer "routine" and "recent".
- (viii)/(ix): evidence of critical role should come from organizational documents and third-party corroboration, with salary comparators from a stated, current data source.
- (v) Open source: gather adoption by unaffiliated parties (dependents, downloads, external citations) rather than relying on letters.
- Timeline: a mid-2027 filing faces no ROW EB-1 queue per the Oct 2026 bulletin; premium processing has produced fast approvals in these anecdotes. Recheck the bulletin before filing.
