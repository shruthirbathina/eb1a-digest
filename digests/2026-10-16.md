## Patterns
- Success stories are self-selected marketing; they show RFEs and prior denials are survivable but not what changed (ANECDOTAL, links above).
- Peer review is cited with counts (15+) in at least one approval (ANECDOTAL).
- Reported approval rate decline (about 75% to 61%, FY2023 to FY2025) comes from one secondary source, unverified: [VisaServe](https://visaserve.com/?p=11688).
- No paid-award, paid-media or predatory-journal cases surfaced.

## Implications for our refile
- (iv): document review counts per venue (SIGIR, KDD, CIKM, RecSys, ACM journals) with invitation records and acceptance-rate/selectivity evidence, across multiple years to answer "routine".
- (v)/(vi): back contribution claims with independent adoption and citation data rather than letters, consistent with the reported letters-as-supporting-evidence stance.
- (viii)/(ix): independent comparators for salary and organizational evidence for the role.
- Re-check the November bulletin when published before fixing a mid-2027 filing date.
